# Acceptable use

Protected file: changes need human review.

## Not allowed, ever

- Anything illegal where the project owner, contributor, or affected people are.
- Malware, exploits, credential harvesting, phishing, or spam tooling.
- Scraping or automation that breaks a site's or service's terms.
- Work on projects you don't own or have no right to change.
- Evading these rules, the gate, or bans (including through new accounts).

## Projects

Must have an open license and a verified owner. Intake screening runs before a project goes live.

## Enforcement

- Automated screening of tasks and PRs. Flagged items wait for a human.
- One strike for clear violations. Bans are tied to GitHub identity.
- Everything is logged. Appeals go to the maintainers; they're narrow.
